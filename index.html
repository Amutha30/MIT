Travel & Transit Options

By Train (Direct Access)

Kundapura Railway Station (KUDA) is located directly opposite the college campus (less than 200 meters walking distance!). Direct trains available from Bengaluru, Mangaluru, Mumbai, Goa, and Kerala.

By Bus (KSRTC & Private)

Kundapura KSRTC and Private Bus Stand are located 3.5 km from the campus. Regular college shuttle buses, autos, and local town buses connect every 10 minutes.

By Air

Mangaluru International Airport (IXE) is 92 km away (approx. 1.5 hours drive via NH-66). Taxis and express buses are readily accessible.
