# ORBIT 2K26 | Moodlakatte Institute of Technology, Kundapura (MITK)

> **Emerging into a New Dimension**  
> Annual 2-Day National Tech & Cultural Conclave  
> **Dates:** October 9th & 10th, 2026  
> **Location:** Moodlakatte Institute of Technology Kundapura (MITK), Udupi District, Karnataka – 576217 (Opp. Kundapura Railway Station)

---

## 🌌 Conclave Highlights
- **42-Hour Continuous Flagship Hackathon (HackOrbit):** 42 continuous hours of problem-solving across AI/ML, Web3, Smart Cities, HealthTech & Agritech. (₹75,000 Prize Pool)
- **VTU 4th-Year Capstone Project Expo & Publication:** Exclusive track for final-year BE/B.Tech students from VTU-affiliated colleges to present projects and receive official publication in **ORBIT 2K26 Conference Proceedings (ISBN registered)** with certificates. (₹35,000 Prize Pool)
- **DevOrbit (Web-a-thon):** Rapid 5-hour responsive full-stack sprint. (₹25,000 Prize Pool)
- **AppOrbit (Mobile-a-thon):** Cross-platform & native mobile app development showdown. (₹25,000 Prize Pool)
- **TechScribe:** National technical paper presentation in IEEE format.
- **CyberArena (E-Sports):** BGMI & Valorant LAN tournament with live auditorium shoutcasting.
- **CosmoQuest:** Campus-wide crypto-treasure hunt across the 20-acre green campus.
- **RhythmOrbit:** Battle of the Bands & acoustic open-air musical night.
- **ByteBattle, RoboClash, BrainOrbit Quiz, Step Up Street Dance, Dimension Cosplay**, and more!

## 🚀 Running Locally

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Amutha30/MIT.git
   cd MIT
   ```

2. **Start the local server:**
   Using Node.js:
   ```bash
   node server.js
   ```
   Or open `index.html` directly in any modern browser!

3. **Open in browser:**
   ```
   http://127.0.0.1:3000
   ```
